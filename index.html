How it works

From breakdown to paid — in eight plain steps

Here's the whole journey, exactly as it happens. Nothing hidden, nothing complicated.

The journey

1
Something breaks

A line stops, a motor burns out, a unit fails. The company needs a specialist — fast.

2
Search Craig

Search by industry, skills or qualifications — “mechanical fitter”, “PLC”, “F-Gas” — and see verified engineers nearby.

3
Contact & book

Message the engineer through Craig and book the days you need, right there on the site.

4
Flat-rate price agreed

The company pays £50 per man, per day. The price is agreed before anyone sets foot on site.

5
Engineer sends a timesheet

When the work's done, the engineer sends their timesheet through Craig — a couple of taps.

6
Craig invoices the company

We raise the invoice for the company. No paperwork for either side to chase.

7
Money held securely for 7 days

The payment sits in a one-week holding window. If there's any issue with the job, there's time to sort it out fairly before money moves.

8
Released to the engineer

After 7 days the money is released to the engineer, minus the £25 service fee charged to the contractor. Everyone can see the full breakdown.

Why the 7-day hold?

It protects everyone. The company gets a fair window to raise a genuine issue. The engineer knows the money is already paid and on its way — no chasing invoices for 30 days. And every payment shows its state clearly: Paid → Held → Released.

About Craig

Built by someone who knows the tools — for people who'd rather be on them

Craig exists because finding a good specialist engineer at short notice is still far harder than it should be — and because good engineers deserve steady work and prompt, visible payment.

The problem we saw

When a machine stops, production stops. Companies ring round agencies, leave voicemails, and lose hours — sometimes days. Meanwhile, skilled independent engineers with exactly the right tickets are sitting ten miles away, invisible.

And when the work does get done, the old way buries everyone in paper: timesheets chased by phone, invoices raised by hand, payment terms stretched to 30 days and beyond.

What Craig does differently

One platform where a company can search by industry, skill or qualification, book a verified engineer the same day, and follow the money in plain sight: £50 per man, per day, invoiced by us, held securely for 7 days so any issue can be resolved fairly, then released to the engineer.

Everything is written in plain English and built big-button simple — because the people who keep Britain's machines running shouldn't need a computer science degree to get booked and paid.

Fast when it matters

Built for the unplanned breakdown — the 6am phone call no plant manager wants.

Honest, flat pricing

One rate, agreed up front. Fees shown on every payout, before the money moves.

People first

Free membership while the community grows, and a 7-day hold that treats both sides fairly.
